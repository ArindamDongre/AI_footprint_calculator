Methodology

Energy estimates follow Luccioni et al. (2023). Carbon intensity by region from Dodge et al. (2022). Water consumption model from Ren et al. (2023). PUE ranges from IEA Electricity 2023. All values shown with min / avg / max uncertainty ranges.

Scope Disclaimer

This tool estimates inference-only environmental costs. Pre-training, fine-tuning, hardware manufacturing (embodied carbon), and scope-3 emissions are explicitly out of scope. Estimates are educational approximations, not audit instruments.
