🌱 Reduce Your AI Footprint

Small changes in how you use AI can make a measurable difference

🔵

Use smaller models for simple tasks

A small LLM uses up to 95% less energy than a large one. Not every question needs GPT-4.

✂️

Keep prompts concise

Very long prompts use 2.5× more energy. Be specific and direct — it's better for you and the planet.

🌿

Choose green providers

A data center on renewable energy emits 14× less carbon than one on a coal-heavy grid.

🤔

Ask: "Do I need AI for this?"

A Google search uses ~0.3 Wh. A ChatGPT query uses ~0.7 Wh. Sometimes search is enough.

📋 Methodology & Sources

Complete documentation of formulas, parameter tables, and citations

📐 Core Formulas

Step 1 — Server-side Energy per Query

Eserver = αm × βl × γh [kWh/query]

Where αm = base energy for model class, βl = prompt-length factor, γh = hardware efficiency factor

Step 2 — Facility Energy (with PUE overhead)

Efacility = Eserver × PUE [kWh/query]

PUE > 1.0 accounts for cooling, power distribution, lighting. Range: 1.10 (hyperscaler) – 1.58 (global avg)

Step 3 — Carbon Emissions

Cquery = Efacility × Igrid [gCO₂e/query]

Igrid = regional carbon intensity (gCO₂e/kWh). Range: 20 (renewable) – 950 (coal-heavy)

Step 4 — Water Usage

Wquery = Efacility × (WUE + EWIF) × 1000 [mL/query]

WUE = on-site cooling water, EWIF = electricity-withdrawal intensity factor (both L/kWh)

Step 5 — Uncertainty Propagation

min = all best-case params  |  avg = all average params  |  max = all worst-case params

⚠️ Scope & Limitations
  • Inference only: Pre-training, fine-tuning, and hardware manufacturing are out of scope.
  • Uncertainty model: Min/avg/max uses independent best/worst-case propagation.
  • Energy anchoring: Calibrated to 2025 disclosures (Google 0.24 Wh, OpenAI 0.34 Wh).
  • Educational tool: Approximations for awareness, not audit-grade measurements.

Methodology

Energy calibrated against Google (2025) and OpenAI (2025) disclosures, with ranges from Luccioni et al. (2023). Carbon from Ember (2025) and CEA India (FY2024-25). Water from Ren et al. (2023). PUE from Uptime Institute (2025). All values with min / avg / max.

Scope

This tool estimates inference-only environmental costs. Pre-training, fine-tuning, hardware manufacturing, and scope-3 emissions are out of scope. Estimates are educational approximations.
